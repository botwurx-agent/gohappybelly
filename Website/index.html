The Five Gut Patterns

Most chronic gut symptoms trace back to one of five root causes.

The free Gut Pattern Quiz identifies which one is driving yours, so you stop guessing and start addressing the right thing.

🌿

The Wired-and-Tired Gut

Exhausted but wired. Energy crashes at predictable times. Stress and gut are deeply tangled.

"The food is fine. The system processing it isn't."
🌾

The Reactive Gut

Reactions to more and more foods. Bloating, inflammation, skin flare-ups. The list of triggers keeps growing.

"The foods aren't the problem. The barrier is."
🍂

The Sugar-Driven Gut

Relentless sugar and carb cravings. Weight that won't move. Mood swings and afternoon crashes.

"The cravings aren't yours. They're something else's."
🌱

The Underactive Gut

Everything feels heavy after meals. Slow digestion, fullness that lingers, low stomach acid.

"The problem isn't what you're eating. It's how it's being broken down."
🌬️

The Bloated and Backed-Up Gut

Flat in the morning, bloated by afternoon. Constipation, recurring symptoms despite doing everything right.

"The bacteria aren't the problem. The location is."
