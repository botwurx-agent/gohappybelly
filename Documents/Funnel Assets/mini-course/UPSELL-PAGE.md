# The $27 → $147 upsell page

**Status:** copy ready. Steve creates the upsell shell, then pastes this in.

## Why the agent did not build this as a landing page

A Kajabi **Upsell** is its own resource type, created at **Sales → Pricing →
Upsells**, with a template that carries the accept button and the decline link.
Those elements are what charge the card already on file. That is the one-click.

The MCP has 19 toolsets and none of them is upsells. It can create landing
pages, and a landing page here would be actively wrong: its button would be an
ordinary link to `/offers/jmFpFL5m/checkout`, the buyer would type her card in
again, and the whole advantage would be gone.

So this is copy, not a build. Steve creates the upsell, clicks **Customize**,
and pastes.

## The timing problem this copy has to solve

The upsell fires **immediately after the $27 checkout**, before she has read a
single lesson.

So the copy cannot say "now that you understand how your gut works." She does
not yet. Anything built on what she has just learned is false at the moment she
reads it. The copy below is built on what she is **about to** find out instead,
which is both true and a better hook: she is ten minutes from a question she
will want the answer to.

## Notes on the choices

**No discount.** Standard practice here is an upsell-only price, and it would
probably lift conversion. It is not in this copy because changing the price of
the $147 is Steve's decision, not the agent's. Worth deciding before launch: a
$127 upsell-only price is the obvious version.

**No countdown, no scarcity.** The offer is evergreen and so is the course.
Inventing urgency on a page sold by a practitioner to women who have already
been let down by people selling them things is a bad trade for a few points of
conversion.

**"You can buy it later at the same price"** is deliberate and it costs
conversion. It is also true, and saying it is what makes the rest of the page
believable. The decline link is written the same way: no shame, no "I don't
want to feel better."

**The reintroduction is the lead feature**, not the four weeks. It is the part
the $27 course explicitly says it cannot give her (module B3, the elimination
passage), so the two offers already point at each other.

## Steve's steps

1. **Sales → Pricing → Upsells → create**, attach the $147 offer
   (`2149370663`).
2. **Customize**, paste the copy above.
3. On the **Gut Foundations** offer (`2151464245`), open **Purchase Flow** and
   add this upsell as step 1.
4. Publish the Gut Foundations offer.

**Name it for the offer it serves**, something like "Gut Reset after Gut
Foundations". Editing an upsell page changes it for every offer it is attached
to, so a generic name invites someone to reuse this one later and silently
rewrite this copy.
