Same Symptom, Different Cause

Here is the thing that keeps people stuck for years.

Bloating is not a cause. Neither is constipation. They are the sound the engine makes, and the same sound can come from five completely different problems. Two women can both say "I bloat after every meal" and need opposite things.

This is why the advice never works. Not because the advice is bad, but because it was written for someone whose bloating came from somewhere else. Cut out FODMAPs when your issue is that your stomach acid is low and you will feel briefly better and then worse, because you narrowed your diet without touching the reason.

So before any protocol, any supplement, any elimination, the question is which pattern you are in.

The Five

In my practice, almost everything sorts into one of five.

The Wired-and-Tired Gut

Your nervous system is stuck in the on position, and digestion only runs when your body believes it is safe. You eat well. You react anyway. You feel worse when life gets loud.

It is a car with the engine timing off. Every part works. They are firing in the wrong sequence.

The food is fine. The system processing it isn't.

The Reactive Gut

The barrier we talked about in module one has stopped being selective. Your list of safe foods keeps getting shorter, and cutting more of them out buys less relief each time.

It is a damaged screen door. The filter is meant to keep things out. The holes are letting them through.

The foods aren't the problem. The barrier is.

The Sugar-Driven Gut

Cravings that do not feel like a choice. Worse after sugar, bread, alcohol. A coating on the tongue, fog that lifts and returns.

It is squatters in the house. Something moved in and worked out how to get the household to keep feeding it.

The cravings aren't yours. They're something else's, broadcasting through you.

The Underactive Gut

Heaviness after meals that sits for hours. Burping. Feeling full on very little. Supplements that seem to do nothing.

It is a kitchen with a stove that is not hot enough. The food goes in the pan and sits there instead of cooking.

The problem isn't what you're eating. It's that your stomach isn't breaking it down.

The Bloated and Backed-Up Gut

Flat in the morning, visibly distended by evening. Reacts to the healthy foods. Gets better on antibiotics or a restrictive diet, then comes back.

It is bacteria that moved to the wrong neighborhood. And it is a flooded basement where nobody fixed the leak, which is why the water keeps coming back.

The bacteria aren't the problem. The location is.

What Symptoms Actually Tell You

Symptoms are the clues, not the answer. What makes them useful is the combination and the timing.

What you notice Worth noting
BloatingWhen it starts, and whether it builds across the day
ConstipationFewer than one movement a day, or hard to pass
CravingsWhether they follow a meal or arrive out of nowhere
Fatigue after eatingHow long after, and after which meals
Skin or mood changesWhether they track with anything you ate

One of those on its own means very little. Three of them clustering in a consistent way starts to point somewhere.

Alina's take. People come to me with a list of foods they have removed. It is usually long, and it usually took years to build. Almost nobody arrives with a list of when their symptoms happen. The second list is worth more than the first, and it costs nothing to start keeping.
