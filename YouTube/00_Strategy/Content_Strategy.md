## 1. Content Pillars

Five pillars the channel produces against. Every long-form video should map to exactly one pillar (per the Packaging Checklist in `Reference_Playbook.md`, Section 7: one clear idea, one archetype or avatar).

### Pillar 1: Root-Cause Pattern Education
Explainer videos that walk through one of the five gut archetypes end to end: what it is, why it happens, and the locked analogy and aha line for that pattern. This is the channel's core evergreen pillar and the most direct search-and-recommendation bridge into the funnel, since it uses the exact "gut pattern" language the quiz already uses.
- **Title formulas used:** numbered list, authority-hijack, STOP imperative (per Playbook Section 1).
- **Overperforming topic ties:** fasting/digestion resets, insulin/blood sugar, cortisol, low stomach acid, bacterial relocation (Playbook Section 5).

### Pillar 2: Symptom-to-Pattern Breakdown
Videos that start from a specific, searchable symptom (bloating after eating, brain fog, fatigue, skin flare-ups) and walk backward to the pattern that could be driving it. This pillar carries the bulk of the plain-language search terms from `Channel_Foundation.md` Section 5.
- **Title formulas used:** curiosity-experiment ("What Happens If..."), numbered list, specific-percentage claim (only with a real, traceable number).
- **Overperforming topic ties:** visceral/belly bloating, anti-aging and skin (Playbook Section 5).

### Pillar 3: Myth-Busting and Consumer Safety
Debunking diet myths, calling out gut-irritating additives or supplement-quality red flags, and covering food-policy or food-safety news through a gut-health lens. Reaches colder, more skeptical audiences, so every video in this pillar must stay strictly in "this pattern often shows up when" language.
- **Title formulas used:** "brands to avoid" numbered list, STOP imperative, news-jacking (used carefully, per Playbook Section 5 caution).
- **Overperforming topic ties:** "brands to avoid" consumer-safety format, news-jacking food policy, creatine/supplement deep-dives.

### Pillar 4: Personal Experiments and Protocol Trials
Alina trying a structured trial on herself (an eating-window experiment, a digestion-support protocol) and reporting the real result. Builds trust and personality-driven watch time, directly using the playbook's proven personal-experiment format.
- **Title formulas used:** personal-experiment ("I tried X for N days").
- **Overperforming topic ties:** fasting/digestion resets, supplement deep-dives.
- **Hard rule:** any number or outcome reported must be Alina's own real trial result, never invented to fit the format (Playbook Section 1, closing rule).

### Pillar 5: Life-Stage and Identity Content
Videos built around a life stage or identity rather than a symptom: perimenopause, postpartum, "after the diets stopped working," being dismissed by a doctor, being the one who finally puts herself first. This pillar carries most of the emotional, nurture-driven content and leans on Hook Pattern 5 (open with the avatar's exact frustration, in her words).
- **Title formulas used:** authority-hijack, curiosity-experiment, numbered list.
- **Overperforming topic ties:** cortisol/stress framing for the caregiver and burned-out-professional avatars, blood-sugar framing for the frustrated-dieter avatar.

## 3. Cadence and Format (locked, referenced from the Playbook)

- **2 long-form uploads per week**, produced through batch-filming, not one-video-per-week filming. *Per `Reference_Playbook.md`, Section 3.*
- **Solo, single-topic videos, 16 to 27 minutes.** No guest interviews for this channel while it builds watch history and authority. *Per `Reference_Playbook.md`, Section 3.*
- **One idea per video**, mapped to exactly one archetype and/or avatar, using the Packaging Checklist before scripting (`Reference_Playbook.md`, Section 7).
- Every video should identify a specific next-video end-screen link and flag its highest-retention segment for short-form repurposing, per method principles 10 and 11 (`Reference_Playbook.md`, Section 6).

## 4. CTA Phasing Plan

### Phase 1: Grow-First (current phase, starts at channel launch)

- **Primary CTA: Subscribe.** Every video and the channel About copy ask for a subscribe, nothing more direct.
- **Quiz link: soft only.** Present in the video description and in a pinned comment ("if you want to know your gut pattern, there's a free quiz linked below"), never argued for on camera as the main call to action.
- **No hard selling.** No on-camera mention of the Gut Breakthrough Call, the 3-Month Gut Reset, Guthub.ai, or any individual test (GI-MAP, SIBO, OAT, etc.) during Phase 1. Education comes before any pitch, matching the playbook's "you have to earn the right to sell something to somebody" principle (`Reference_Playbook.md`, Section 6, item 13).
- **Rationale:** the channel has zero watch history and zero subscriber trust at launch. Every method principle in the playbook (recommendation-engine-first, consistency, hook-as-ad, education-before-pitch) points to spending the first stretch of the channel's life building a library and a recognizable identity, not converting cold viewers.

### Phase 2: Quiz-First, Then Offer Ladder (with traction)

Once the channel has real traction, CTAs escalate in this order, still never skipping ahead of education in any single video:

1. **Quiz becomes the primary CTA**, stated directly on camera at the end of relevant videos ("if this sounds like you, take the free quiz to find your pattern"), not just described softly in the description.
2. **Guthub.ai ($13/month)** introduced as the natural next step after the quiz for viewers who want ongoing support at a low commitment, positioned as the entry offer it already is in the funnel.
3. **Gut Breakthrough Call** (free, 20-minute) introduced as the CTA for videos targeting the highest-urgency avatars (Avatar 1 The Dismissed Patient, Avatar 6 The Post-Menopausal Woman, Avatar 7 The Caregiver), consistent with the existing funnel architecture (quiz leads to email sequence leads to call leads to the $1,497 3-Month Gut Reset).

**Proposed trigger to move from Phase 1 to Phase 2** (proposed, not locked; Steve should confirm or adjust based on actual traction once the channel is live):

> Move to Phase 2 once the channel reaches **1,000 subscribers**, OR once the most recent 8 uploads (roughly one month at the 2/week cadence) are **averaging 5,000+ views each**, whichever happens first.

This mirrors the playbook's data-driven posture: track real retention and view data (`Reference_Playbook.md`, Section 6, item 9) and let actual performance, not a launch-date calendar, decide when the channel has enough trust and reach to carry a heavier CTA.
