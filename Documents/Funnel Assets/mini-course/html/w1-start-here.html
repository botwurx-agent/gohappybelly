Welcome. I am glad you are here.

If you are reading this, something about your digestion has been bothering you long enough that you went looking for answers. Maybe it is bloating that shows up by the afternoon no matter what you ate. Maybe it is the fatigue, the brain fog, the skin that flares for no reason you can name. Maybe someone already told you your labs were normal and you should try to stress less.

This course is the groundwork. It is not a protocol and it is not personalized, because I have not met you. What it does is give you the map: how digestion actually works, what it is connected to, and what the common patterns look like when it stops working well.

Here is how to use it. There are eight modules. Each one takes about ten minutes. Each ends with one question for you to answer, and those questions are the part most people skip and should not, because the answers are what turn this from information into something you can act on.

Work through them in order. You can finish in an afternoon, but you will get more out of it spreading it across a week and paying attention to your own body in between.
