## Lesson: Start Here

Welcome. I am glad you are here.

If you are reading this, something about your digestion has been bothering you
long enough that you went looking for answers. Maybe it is bloating that shows
up by the afternoon no matter what you ate. Maybe it is the fatigue, the brain
fog, the skin that flares for no reason you can name. Maybe someone already told
you your labs were normal and you should try to stress less.

This course is the groundwork. It is not a protocol and it is not personalized,
because I have not met you. What it does is give you the map: how digestion
actually works, what it is connected to, and what the common patterns look like
when it stops working well.

Here is how to use it. There are eight modules. Each one takes about ten
minutes. Each ends with one question for you to answer, and those questions are
the part most people skip and should not, because the answers are what turn this
from information into something you can act on.

Work through them in order. You can finish in an afternoon, but you will get
more out of it spreading it across a week and paying attention to your own body
in between.

## Lesson: Who Is Teaching This

I trained as a Functional Diagnostic Nutrition Practitioner because I was tired
of watching women get handed something for a symptom nobody had bothered to
explain.

Almost everyone I work with arrives having already been told their labs are
normal. Several have been told it is stress, or hormones, or their age. A few
have been told it is in their head.

Functional diagnostics looks at how your systems are performing rather than
whether you have crossed a disease threshold. That is a different question, and
it needs different testing to answer. It is also why I will tell you plainly
when something belongs with your doctor rather than with me. I am not a
physician and I do not diagnose.

What I do is find the pattern and build the work around it. This course is the
first half of that: understanding the system well enough that the pattern, when
you find it, actually means something to you.

## Lesson: What I Can And Cannot Do Here

I want to be straightforward with you about this up front, because it matters.

I am a Functional Diagnostic Nutrition Practitioner. I am not a medical doctor.
I do not diagnose conditions and I do not prescribe. What I do is look at
patterns, the way symptoms cluster and what tends to sit underneath them, and
help people work with the pattern they are in.

So you will notice I never say "you have" anything in this course. I say "this
pattern often shows up when." That is not hedging. It is the honest description
of what can be known from the outside.

Taking this course does not make you my client, and nothing here replaces
individual medical care. If you take a medication, decisions about it belong
with the person who prescribed it. If something in here raises a question about
your own health, that is a good thing, and the right next step is a
conversation, not a guess.
