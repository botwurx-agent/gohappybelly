# Course diagrams

Hand-built SVG, rendered with Playwright. **Not generated, not stock.** For
anything inside the body, where a camera cannot go and a photograph cannot
substitute. Direction and the photograph/illustration split:
`Website/IMAGE-DIRECTION.md`.

    node render.mjs        # lesson-01.html -> 01-tract.png, 01-villi.png, 01-barrier.png

Run with node; the export step below needs **`python3.13`**, since the system
`python3` has a broken PIL in this container.

## Why these are built rather than generated

Three things have to be exact, and a generative model gives none of them
reliably: the palette, the labels, and the anatomy. A diagram with a garbled
word or a wrong organ is worse than no diagram. Same reasoning as the course
mockups: build it when precision matters, generate it when atmosphere matters.

## What they replaced, and why it mattered

The originals were **stock medical illustrations**, not cut paper, so they were
never part of what Alina rejected. They failed for their own reasons:

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Clinical reds and pinks against a sage and cream system | Website palette throughout |
| `diagram-digestive-anatomy.png` **showed a male body** on a product whose seven avatars are all women | No body at all. The tract is drawn as one continuous tube, which is also exactly what the caption claims |
| Labels read "Paracellular", "Transcellular", "Normal tight junction" | "Mouth", "Stomach", "Sealed", "Loosened", "inside the tube", "bloodstream" |

## The three

| File | Shows | Teaching point |
|---|---|---|
| `l01-tract.png` | Mouth to exit as a single unbroken tube, four labelled zones | The continuity *is* the lesson. Drawing it as one stroke makes the point the stock anatomy image could not |
| `l01-villi.png` | Smooth lining beside folded lining, same wall length | Surface area, shown rather than asserted |
| `l01-barrier.png` | Sealed cells beside loosened ones, nutrients crossing through vs larger pieces slipping between | The screen door, made literal. The core mechanism of the whole course |

## Build notes

- **Draw the large intestine as one path.** The first version used two
  (colon, then rectum) with `opacity: 0.88`, and the overlap at the join stacked
  into a visibly darker seam. One continuous stroke at full opacity instead.
- Repetitive parts (villi fingers, cells, particles) are generated by a short
  inline script rather than hand-written, so spacing stays even and the file
  stays legible.
- Rendered with `omitBackground: true`, so the PNGs carry alpha and sit on
  whatever the lesson wraps them in.
- The first tract was 1000x1210 and far too tall for a lesson column. Check the
  aspect against the column it lands in, not just the canvas.

## The hero is a photograph, not a diagram

`l01-hero-sieve.jpg` replaces the cut-paper sieve. The analogy stays, the style
changes: a real wire sieve with oats held on the mesh and fine flour falling
through. That is the allocation rule working as intended, and it is why the
analogy images do not all need inventing from scratch.
