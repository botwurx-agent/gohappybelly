# Shoot Day Checklist: YT-005 (First Video)

Everything needed to film and upload the first Go Happy Belly video. Print this or keep it open on set.

## 1. Before the shoot
- [ ] Alina reads the script out loud twice. Goal is familiarity, not memorization. She should be able to say each section in her own words if needed.
- [ ] Mark the 3 lines that must land word for word: the hook ("the bloating was never really about the food"), the aha line ("The food is fine. The system processing it isn't."), and the closing ("You're not broken. Your timing is just off.").
- [ ] Wardrobe: solid, warm, on-brand tones (cream, sage, terracotta, navy). Avoid busy patterns and pure white or pure black (they fight the camera). No large logos.
- [ ] Location: quiet room, soft daylight or a key light, a clean but warm background (not clinical). A plant, a shelf, warm wall. Nothing medical.
- [ ] Water nearby, phone on silent, notifications off.

## 2. Gear and setup
- [ ] Camera at eye level, framed chest-up with a little headroom. Alina's eyes on the top third line.
- [ ] Lighting: main light slightly to one side and in front, softened (window with a sheer, or a softbox). Avoid overhead-only light (raccoon shadows).
- [ ] Audio is the priority. Use a lav mic or a shotgun mic close to her, not the camera's built-in mic. Record a 10-second silent room tone for the editor.
- [ ] Teleprompter: use a phone/tablet teleprompter app just below or over the lens so her eyeline stays on camera. Set scroll speed slow enough for natural pauses.
- [ ] Do a 20-second test recording. Check focus, exposure, and audio levels before the real take.

## 3. Delivery notes for Alina
- [ ] Talk to one person, not an audience. Imagine one specific woman who has unbuttoned her pants at her desk.
- [ ] Slow down on the hook and the aha line. Pauses read as confidence, not mistakes.
- [ ] Energy slightly higher than feels natural. Camera flattens energy by about 20 percent.
- [ ] It is fine to restart a sentence. Pause, clap once (helps the editor find the cut), and redo the line.
- [ ] Warm and direct, never clinical. If a line feels stiff, say it the way she would say it to a client.

## 4. Shot list (what to capture)
**A-roll (the main talking):** film each section as its own take so editing is easy: Hook, Credential, Problem, Point 1, Point 2, Point 3, Point 4, Point 5, Bridge/CTA, Outro.

**B-roll to grab (10 to 20 seconds each, silent):** the editor uses these over the audio.
- [ ] A woman at a desk quietly unbuttoning a waistband, hand on stomach (hook)
- [ ] A fridge with taped-up "safe food" lists, a symptom journal (problem block)
- [ ] Alina demonstrating the sit-down, fork-down, slow exhale before eating (Point 5, film this clearly, it is also a clip)
- [ ] A warm bowl of soup next to a big raw salad (Point 4, the warm-versus-raw sign)
- [ ] Optional: hands making a slow calming gesture, a candle or tea, warm cozy cues for the calm-gear moments
- [ ] The engine-timing sketch can be an animation added in post, no need to film it.
