## Check the mechanism, not the renamed label

Steve caught this and it was a real miss. The five names are **renames of
clinical mechanisms**, and an image can match the new name while contradicting
the thing underneath it.

The first Sugar-Driven pass had her reaching for **glass jars of oats and
lentils**. That matches "reaching into a cupboard" and flatly contradicts
microbial overgrowth, whose whole signature is a craving for sugar and refined
carbohydrate, and whose aha line is "the cravings aren't yours, they're
something else's". The picture argued against its own caption. Fixed: the shelf
now carries an open tin of biscuits and a chocolate bar with the foil peeled
back, her hand closing on the chocolate, with the healthy jars in the
foreground being reached over. The reaching-past is the point.

Underactive had the same problem more quietly: a plate of rice, chickpeas and
flatbread. Low stomach acid shows up heaviest on **protein**, so the abandoned
plate is now a barely cut-into piece of roast chicken.

**Rule: art-direct from the mechanism in CLAUDE.md, then check the finished
frame against the aha line.** If the picture and the line disagree, the picture
is wrong.

## The three direction rules this model needs

Nano Banana 2.1 is more photographic than GPT Image 2.5 and a weaker director.
Left alone it reverts to stock photography. Every prompt needs, explicitly:

1. **"She is looking away from the camera, never at the lens."** Without it,
   both bake-off frames came back as lens-contact portraits.
2. **"She is NOT touching her stomach,"** plus the gesture that is wanted
   instead. Without it, both bake-off frames put a hand flat on the abdomen,
   which is the exact stock cliche the symptom-moment brief exists to avoid.
3. **Blank packaging, stated object by object.** It is a weak instruction
   follower on text. "No readable text" twice was not enough; the packet had to
   be described as unprinted kraft with no nutrition panel and no barcode.
   Check every frame.

## And the trap from the earlier GPT set, still live

Put the palette in the **room and the light, never the clothing**. A shared
style suffix naming sage green produced five photographs of what looked like
one woman in one sweater. Name a different garment, age and woman per prompt.

## Compliance

Nobody is diagnosed. These are moments, not symptoms attributed to a named
condition, and the pattern framing lives in the copy beside them. The mechanism
names in the table above are **art direction only** and must never reach the
page. Alt text describes what is in the photograph, never what the reader has.
