Everything needed to film and upload this video. Print this or keep it open on set.

## 1. Before the shoot
- [ ] **Alina has signed off on the 4 [ALINA CONFIRM] lines in the script** (credential "two, three, sometimes five doctors"; "second week is usually when women notice it easing"; the 3 mistakes in Point 6; the overall reset description). Any line she cannot confirm gets cut or reworded before the teleprompter file is made. No unconfirmed claim goes on camera.
- [ ] Teleprompter file made from the approved script: [SPOKEN] lines only, no brackets, no notes.
- [ ] Alina reads the script out loud twice. Goal is familiarity, not memorization.
- [ ] Mark the 3 lines that must land word for word: the hook ("Here is the real reason that happens, and it is not that you missed a trigger food"), the aha line ("The bacteria aren't the problem. The location is."), and the close ("You did not fail the diet, and you are not the problem. The location was.").
- [ ] Wardrobe: solid, warm, on-brand tones (cream, sage, terracotta, navy). Avoid busy patterns and pure white or pure black. No large logos. **Bring a second top** so YT-005 (filmed the same day) does not look identical in a suggested-videos grid.
- [ ] Location: quiet room, soft daylight or a key light, a clean but warm background (not clinical). A plant, a shelf, warm wall. Nothing medical.
- [ ] Water nearby, phone on silent, notifications off.
- [ ] **Thumbnail photos:** 10 minutes of stills at the start of the session while hair, makeup, and energy are fresh (see section 4).

## 2. Gear and setup
- [ ] Camera at eye level, framed chest-up with a little headroom. Alina's eyes on the top third line.
- [ ] Lighting: main light slightly to one side and in front, softened (window with a sheer, or a softbox). Avoid overhead-only light. If using window light, note the time; it changes across a long batch day.
- [ ] Audio is the priority. Use a lav mic or a shotgun mic close to her, not the camera's built-in mic. Record a 10-second silent room tone for the editor.
- [ ] Teleprompter: use a phone/tablet teleprompter app just below or over the lens so her eyeline stays on camera. Set scroll speed slow enough for natural pauses.
- [ ] Do a 20-second test recording. Check focus, exposure, and audio levels before the real take.
- [ ] Spare batteries and a second memory card. A batch day eats both.

## 3. Delivery notes for Alina
- [ ] Talk to one person, not an audience. Imagine one specific woman who has already tried the elimination diet and is still bloated.
- [ ] This video carries more validation weight than usual. She has likely been dismissed by more than one doctor. Let the warmth sit before the direct lines land.
- [ ] Slow down on the hook, the aha line, the "normal labs, wrong map" line, and "It was not the wrong brand. It was the wrong question." Pauses read as confidence, not mistakes.
- [ ] Energy slightly higher than feels natural. Camera flattens energy by about 20 percent.
- [ ] It is fine to restart a sentence. Pause, clap once (helps the editor find the cut), and redo the line.
- [ ] Warm and direct, never clinical. This video's emotional job is to take the self-blame off the table ("you did not fail the diet").
- [ ] Reminder: the 14-day reset described in this script is educational content Alina teaches, not a personal challenge she has to run first. No BTS challenge footage needed. This video is shoot-ready as a straight teaching video.
- [ ] Do not skip the safety line in Point 5 ("blood in your stool, weight loss you cannot explain, a fever, or severe pain... see your doctor first"). It protects viewers and the channel.
