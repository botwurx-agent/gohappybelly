Kajabi course `2148514926` ("Go Happy Belly Course"), site `2148204891`.

Done 2026-10-13. 8 modules / 46 lessons, all `action: "updated"` (nothing

## Why

The course was organised around five clinical labels (Leaky Gut, SIBO,
Candida, Low Stomach Acid, Gut Dysbiosis) while the brand, the quiz and
every email are organised around five patterns. Inside and outside did
not agree, and several lesson titles broke the CLAUDE.md guardrails
outright ("How To Heal Leaky Gut", "11 Signs You Have Leaky Gut").

## Pattern mapping

| Course label (old) | Brand pattern (new) | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Leaky Gut | The Reactive Gut | exact (inflammation / permeability) |
| SIBO | The Bloated and Backed-Up Gut | exact |
| Candida Overgrowth | The Sugar-Driven Gut | exact |
| Low Stomach Acid | The Underactive Gut | exact (hypochlorhydria) |
| Gut Dysbiosis | The Wired-and-Tired Gut | **approximate, see below** |

Four map one to one. The fifth does not: the brand's Wired-and-Tired is
nervous system / HPA axis, and the course's Gut Dysbiosis is microbial
imbalance. It took that slot because it is the only one left and because
its body already carries the largest stress / cortisol / boundaries
section of the five protocols. **Its protocol lesson still leads on
microbial balance, not the nervous system. Needs Alina's content pass.**

## MCP mechanics (hard-won)

- `update_course_content` needs `site_id` + `product_id` + `modules`.
- **All ids must be STRINGS**, not integers. Same quirk as `settings_data`.
- `modules` is **upsert keyed on id**, verified empirically: a partial
  send touching one module left the other seven intact. An entry with no
  `id` is treated as a new module and fails on "Title can't be blank".
- `position` is accepted but Kajabi rebalances its sparse integers on
  every write. Array order is what actually decides order, so send the
  array in the order you want.
- **`body` is MARKDOWN, not HTML.** This cost two bad writes. HTML is
  stripped wholesale and the whole thing is flattened into a single `<p>`
  with collapsed newlines, which renders as an unbroken wall of text.
  `## ` becomes `<h2>`, `### ` becomes `<h3>`, `**x**` becomes `<strong>`,
  blank lines separate paragraphs, `- ` builds a `<ul>`. The existing
  rich HTML bodies were authored in the Kajabi admin editor, which is a
  different write path. Always read the lesson back after writing a body.
