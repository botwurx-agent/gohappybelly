## MY ERROR: created lessons lost their HTML (found by Steve, fixed 2026-10-20)

**What happened.** `update_course_content` strips HTML from a lesson body when the lesson is being
CREATED. Headings, lists and paragraph tags were removed and the whole body collapsed into a single
<p> with bare newlines, which browsers do not render. Every lesson I created this session rendered
as one run-on paragraph. Updating an EXISTING lesson preserves HTML perfectly.

**How it got past me.** I verified that every write returned success and that the course structure
was correct. I never re-read the body of a lesson I had created. Steve found it by reading the
course as a member would.

**Fixed by re-sending each affected body as an update.** 15 lessons:
2201292321 (When To Stop And See Your Doctor), 2201292342 (Week 3 pattern page),
2201292365-369 (five shopping lists), 2201292974-979 (five meal plans),
2201293986/987/988 (the three Reintroduction Fortnight lessons).

Not affected, because they were created and then later updated with a new body:
2201292320 (How This Course Works), 2201292374 (Supplement Reference), 2201292375 (When You Have
Finished). All three verified as correct HTML.

**Verified after the repair** by re-reading bodies, not by trusting the success response:
2201292321 and 2201292320 both confirmed as proper HTML with headings and list markup intact.

**Rule for anyone working on this course through the API:** after creating a lesson, always send a
second call that updates its body, and re-read it. A success response means the row saved, not that
the markup survived.
