Why most people don't finish

Most people who try a bone broth fast quit by hour six

Not because they aren't disciplined. Because nobody told them what they were walking into.

  • They don't know how much broth, water, or electrolytes to actually have
  • They get scared by normal adjustment symptoms and stop
  • They have no one to talk them through the hard hours
  • They break the fast wrong and undo the benefits on day four
The shift

Your gut doesn't need punishment. It needs a pause.

Think of your digestive system like a kitchen that has been running service every day for years, no closing hours, no deep clean. Three days of broth isn't starvation. It's the first time in a long time that the kitchen gets to stop, reset, and be properly restocked with minerals, collagen, and the building blocks your gut lining uses to repair itself.

The goal isn't to eat less. It's to give your system a window where it isn't busy.

Before you ask

The questions I get every time

Won't I lose muscle in three days?

Three days is a short window, and bone broth supplies protein and amino acids throughout. This is not a water fast. That said, if you are training hard or already underweight, this round isn't the right fit and I'd rather you sit it out.

Isn't this just detox nonsense?

I'm not going to tell you your body needs help detoxing, because you have a liver and kidneys that handle that. What a short broth reset does is far simpler: it gives your digestive system a stretch of time where it isn't processing a constant stream of food, while still supplying minerals and collagen. That's the whole premise. No mystery, no claims about toxins.

What can I actually have?

Bone broth, water, herbal tea, and electrolytes. The guide gives you exact amounts and timing. We cover the full list on the kickoff call, including the handful of things people always ask about, like black coffee.

Can I make my own broth or do I need to buy it?

Either works. The guide includes recipes with ratios and simmer times if you want to make it, and sourcing guidance on what to look for if you'd rather buy it. Plenty of people do a mix.

What if I have to stop partway through?

Then you stop and you eat. That's not failure, that's paying attention. You keep the guide, the group, and the call replays, and you'll know far more about your own system than you did before. Come to the closing call anyway.

What if I can't make the live calls?

Both calls are recorded and posted in the group. The daily check-ins arrive by email either way. Live is better because you can ask questions in the moment, but the reset works if you catch up on replay.

Is this a diagnosis or a treatment plan?

No. I'm an FDN-P, not a doctor, and nothing here diagnoses or treats a condition. This is a structured, supported group reset built on pattern work. If you have a diagnosed condition or you're under a doctor's care, talk to them before joining.

What happens after the three days?

On the closing call we talk about reintroducing food over the following 24 to 48 hours, and about where to go next if you want to keep going. For some people that's the Gut Pattern quiz. For others it's a Breakthrough Call to talk about working together one to one. No pressure either way.

Please read this before you join

This is not medical advice, and it is not for everyone. Please sit this round out, or loop in your doctor first, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, diabetic, underweight, or have a history of disordered eating.

If at any point during the three days you feel dizzy, faint, or unwell, stop and eat. That instruction stands above anything else on this page.

If you join and then realize you fall into one of these categories, email us and we'll refund you in full, no questions.
