# YouTube Production Protocol

> The repeatable system for getting from backlog idea to published video, every week, without reinventing the process.
> Created 2026-11-17 for the channel launch. Owner: Steve. Built on `Reference_Playbook.md` (cadence, packaging checklist, method principles) and `Content_Strategy.md` (CTA phasing).
> Rule of thumb: **2 long-form videos a week, filmed in batches of 4 every 2 weeks, with 4 approved scripts always waiting in the queue.**

---

## 1. Who does what

| Role | Person | Owns | Does not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Producer** | Steve | Picking topics, reviewing packages, scheduling shoots, approving edits, uploading and publishing, metrics review | Changing clinical content without Alina |
| **Talent + clinical sign-off** | Alina | Reviewing each script for accuracy and voice, confirming every [ALINA CONFIRM] line, filming, replying to comments | Packaging, editing, uploading |
| **Editor** | TBD (or Steve) | Cutting the video, captions, overlays, b-roll, animations, chapters, exporting Shorts | Publishing |
| **Packaging** | Claude (this agent) | Scripts, thumbnail briefs and mockups, repurposing plans, shoot checklists, upload copy, production packet PDFs, backlog and tracker upkeep | Posting, uploading, scheduling, or contacting anyone. Nothing goes live without Steve |

**The hard gate:** nothing is filmed until Alina signs off on the script, and nothing is published until Steve approves the edit.

## 2. The 2-week cycle

Every cycle produces 4 videos (2 a week). Cycles overlap: while one batch is being edited and published, the next batch is being scripted.

| Day | Steve | Alina | Editor | Claude |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon, wk 1** | Picks the next 4 topics from the P1 backlog | | | Builds 4 full packages (script, thumbnail brief, repurpose plan, shoot checklist, packet PDF) |
| **Wed, wk 1** | Reviews packages, sends notes | | | Revises |
| **Thu, wk 1** | Sends approved packets to Alina | Reviews scripts, fills in sign-off sheets (about 20 min per script) | | Applies Alina's edits, makes teleprompter files |
| **Mon or Tue, wk 2** | **BATCH DAY: films 4 videos** (section 4) | **BATCH DAY** | | |
| **Wk 2 to wk 3** | Reviews first cuts within 48 hours | | Edits, about 2 videos a week, 5 to 7 day turnaround each | Final thumbnail mockups, final upload copy with real chapter times |
| **Tue + Fri, ongoing** | **Publishes** (section 6) | Replies to comments for 48 hours | Exports Shorts | Logs metrics in the tracker |

**Queue rule:** keep 4 Alina-approved scripts waiting at all times. If the queue drops below 4, the next Monday is a packaging day, no matter what.

## 3. Stage gates (each stage is done only when its "done when" is true)

### Gate 1: Topic pick
- **Input:** `02_Backlog/Video_Backlog.md`, P1 rows. Challenge videos (YT-002, 007, 013, 015) need lead time, so schedule the challenge first and the shoot after it.
- **Done when:** 4 IDs are chosen and each passes the Packaging Checklist in `Reference_Playbook.md` section 7.

### Gate 2: Package
- **Output per video:** `03_Videos/YT-###_slug/` with `script.md`, `thumbnail-brief.md`, `repurpose.md`, `shoot-checklist.md`, and `YT-###_Production-Packet.pdf`.
- **Runtime rule (new, from the launch review):** Alina speaks about 140 to 150 words per minute. The playbook floor of 16 minutes needs **at least 2,200 spoken words**. Count only the [SPOKEN] lines. A script under 2,200 is not done.
- **Hook rule:** the hook is 35 seconds or less. No intro sting, no logo, no "hey guys" before it.
- **Claims rule:** every statement about Alina's clients or practice results is marked **[ALINA CONFIRM]**. No invented numbers, ever.
- **End-screen rule:** the outro teases a video that is already live or is in the same batch. Otherwise, write the fallback subscribe-only outro.
- **Safety rule:** any video that teaches an eating change includes a short red-flag line (see your doctor first for blood in stool, unexplained weight loss, fever, or severe pain). The description carries the "educational, not medical advice, not a doctor" line.
- **Done when:** compliance checklist at the bottom of the script is fully ticked (non-diagnostic, no em dashes, no banned words, Phase 1 CTA).

### Gate 3: Steve review
- **Done when:** Steve has approved the title, thumbnail direction, and script, or sent notes that have been applied.

### Gate 4: Alina sign-off
- Alina gets the packet PDF plus a one-page sign-off sheet listing only the [ALINA CONFIRM] lines and a voice check. Model: page 2 of `03_Videos/YT-016_.../first-video-brief.md`.
- **Done when:** every [ALINA CONFIRM] line is confirmed, rewritten, or cut, and her wording edits are in the script. Due at least 48 hours before the batch day.

### Gate 5: Shoot prep (the day before)
- Teleprompter file per video: [SPOKEN] lines only, no tags or notes.
- One combined b-roll list for the whole batch, so props get set up once.
- A different top for each video (they sit side by side in suggested feeds).
- Charge batteries, clear memory cards, test the mic.
- **Done when:** the gear check in each shoot checklist (section 2) is ticked.

### Gate 6: Batch day
See section 4.
- **Done when:** all footage is backed up in 2 places and each video is marked `filmed` in the backlog.

### Gate 7: Edit
**Editor standards (every video):**
- Cold open on the hook, no intro animation. Lower third with "Alina Nazari, FDN-P" at the credential moment.
- Burned-in or uploaded captions (a large share of viewers watch muted).
- On-screen text overlays from the shoot checklist, in the brand palette (navy, terracotta, sage, cream). Georgia or a clean serif.
- A visual change (b-roll, overlay, zoom punch-in, or animation) at least every 20 to 30 seconds, especially in the first 2 minutes.
- Keep pauses on the aha lines. Cut every other dead air.
- Analogies (house, basement, engine, and so on) get simple, non-clinical animations. No organ diagrams that imply disease.
- Export: 4K or 1080p master, plus each Shorts clip from `repurpose.md` in 9:16 with captions.
- **Done when:** Steve approves the final cut.

### Gate 8: Thumbnail and title lock
- 2 thumbnail versions built from the brief using a real still of Alina from the batch day. Check both at phone size.
- **Done when:** Steve picks one. Keep the other for a later A/B test (YouTube "Test and compare").

### Gate 9: Publish
See section 6.

### Gate 10: After publishing
See section 7.

## 4. Batch day run of show (4 videos)

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:45 | Setup: camera, light, mic, teleprompter, test recording, room tone |
| 0:45 to 1:05 | **Thumbnail stills for all 4 videos** (change tops between sets). Fresh face, best energy |
| 1:05 to 2:05 | Video A (section by section; hook filmed first and again at the end) |
| 2:05 to 2:20 | Break, change top |
| 2:20 to 3:20 | Video B |
| 3:20 to 4:00 | Lunch |
| 4:00 to 5:00 | Video C |
| 5:00 to 5:15 | Break, change top |
| 5:15 to 6:15 | Video D |
| 6:15 to 6:45 | B-roll pass for all 4 (one combined list), backup footage x2 |

**Order:** film the most important video first while energy is highest. Put the hardest script second (warmed up, not tired yet).
**First batch only:** plan for 4, require 2. Alina has never filmed long-form for this channel, so if energy drops after video 2, stop. Two good videos beat four flat ones.

## 5. File and naming conventions
- Video folders: `03_Videos/YT-###_slug/` (slug = title in lowercase with hyphens).
- Footage: `YT-###_A-roll_[section].mp4`, `YT-###_B-roll_[description].mp4`, `YT-###_thumb-stills/`.
- Edits: `YT-###_cut-v1.mp4`, `YT-###_cut-v2.mp4`, `YT-###_FINAL.mp4`.
- Backlog status: `idea` → `scripted` → `packaged` → `approved` (Alina signed off) → `filmed` → `edited` → `published`.

## 6. Publish checklist (Steve, every upload)
- [ ] Title exactly as approved (60 characters or fewer)
- [ ] Description pasted from the shoot checklist, **chapter times updated to the final cut** (first chapter 0:00, at least 3 chapters)
- [ ] Disclaimer line present in the description
- [ ] Tags pasted
- [ ] Thumbnail uploaded (the approved version)
- [ ] Added to its archetype playlist and its avatar/theme playlist (`Channel_Foundation.md` section 4)
- [ ] End screen: subscribe + the one named next video (only if it is live)
- [ ] Cards: none in Phase 1, or one card to the next video at the outro
- [ ] Audience: "not made for kids"
- [ ] Scheduled for the standard slot (Tuesday and Friday, same time each week; pick a time and keep it)
- [ ] Pinned comment posted the minute it goes live
- [ ] If this video is the end-screen target of an earlier video, update that earlier video's end screen today

---

## 7. After publishing
- **First 48 hours:** Alina (or Steve in her voice, approved) replies to every comment. Per the playbook, creator replies drive subscribes.
- **Shorts:** release clips per the video's `repurpose.md`, one every 2 to 3 days, Instagram first. Steve confirms before anything posts.
- **Day 7 and Day 28:** log in `04_Published/Channel_Tracker.md`: views, click-through rate, average view duration, retention at 1:00 (target 70%), subscribers gained, top comment themes.
- **Diagnose with two numbers:** low click-through rate means the title or thumbnail needs work (try the A/B option). Low retention at 1:00 means the hook needs work. Log the lesson in the tracker's notes column.

## 8. Monthly review (first Monday of the month, 30 minutes)
- Which video overperformed the channel average? Make more like it. Move its siblings in the backlog to P1.
- Update `Reference_Playbook.md` section 4 (hook hypotheses) with real retention data.
- Check the Phase 2 trigger: 1,000 subscribers, or the last 8 uploads averaging 5,000+ views. If hit, Steve decides whether to move CTAs to quiz-first (`Content_Strategy.md` section 4).
- Top up the backlog so there are at least 8 P1 ideas.
