## Filming Basics (all Reels)

- Phone vertical (9:16), at eye level on a tripod, about an arm and a half away. Frame from mid-chest up.
- Face a window for natural light. No overhead light on, no window behind her.
- Clip-on mic if you have one. If not, film in a small room with soft furnishings (less echo).
- Same outfit for every Reel in the session is fine. Solid color, no busy patterns. Sage, cream, navy or terracotta tones match the brand.
- Read each line, then look up and say it to the lens. Paraphrasing is fine as long as the bold aha line stays word for word.
- Do 2 or 3 takes of every hook. The hook matters more than anything else in the video.
- Leave one second of silence before and after each take so it's easy to cut.

**Film in one session:** Posts 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8. Film Post 9 at the end of week 2. Posts 2 and 6 are carousels Steve designs.

## Compliance Check (every post)

- Pattern language only: "this pattern", "this often shows up when". Never "you have SIBO / candida / leaky gut".
- Never say heal, cure or treat. Say address, work with, understand.
- No outcome promises about the course ("you'll be bloat-free"). Promise clarity: "you'll know what you're working with".
- No em dashes in captions or on-screen text.
- Don't assume she's had labs or seen doctors. This audience mostly hasn't.

## Post 1: The Drawer

**Week 1, Monday · Reel · 45 to 50 seconds · Phase: Give (No sell)**

- **Who it's for:** Burned-Out Professional (28-40), Health-Conscious Optimizer (30-50)
- **Pattern:** None specific. This is the shared pain: trying things that never add up.
- **Job of this post:** Stop the scroll, make her feel seen, get comments. No selling.
- **CTA:** None. Ask a question for the comments.

### Setup

- Location: kitchen or bathroom, next to a drawer or cabinet.
- Prop: a drawer or shelf with 8 to 15 supplement bottles, some half-used. Real bottles from the house are best. Turn labels away from camera (no brand names on screen).
- Shot 1 is a close-up of the drawer opening (film it separately). Everything else is Alina to camera, standing beside it.
- Energy: calm, a little knowing, like she's letting the viewer in on something.

### Script

**0:00-0:03 · Hook**

> If you have a drawer that looks like this, I need you to hear something.

- On screen: `Do you have a drawer like this?`
- Visual: Close-up: drawer slides open, bottles visible. Cut to Alina on "I need you to hear something".

**0:03-0:18 · The pain**

> Half-finished probiotics. The magnesium a friend swore by. The digestive enzyme that helped for about two weeks. And every time one stops working, you go buy the next one.

- On screen: `It helped for two weeks. Then it stopped.`
- Visual: Alina picks up 2 or 3 bottles one at a time as she names them, then sets them down.

**0:18-0:38 · The reframe**

> You haven't failed at any of this. You've been guessing, because nobody ever explained what you're actually working with. When you change three things in the same week and you feel a bit better, you have no idea which one mattered. So nothing ever adds up.

- On screen: `You're not failing. You're guessing.`
- Visual: Straight to camera. Slight lean in on "You haven't failed".

**0:38-0:46 · Close (no sell)**

> So tell me in the comments: how many bottles are in yours? Be honest.

- On screen: `How many are in yours?`
- Visual: Small smile. Hold eye contact for a beat after the line, then cut.

**Aha line (word for word):** You're not failing. You're guessing.

**Alternate hook to test:** "This drawer is the most expensive guessing game in your house."

### B-roll

- Drawer opening (2 to 3 takes, slow and normal speed).
- Hand picking up a bottle, turning it, putting it back.
- Optional: bottles lined up on the counter, Alina sliding them aside.

**Reel cover text:** THE DRAWER EVERY WOMAN WITH GUT ISSUES HAS

### Caption

The drawer of half-finished bottles. The thing that helped for two weeks and then stopped.

That's not a willpower problem. It's what happens when nobody tells you what you're actually working with.

How many bottles are in yours? 👇

## Post 3: Bloated by Evening

### Setup

- Location: anywhere calm. Couch or kitchen counter.
- Optional prop: a glass of water or a mug, to make it feel like a chat.
- Two hand gestures matter: one finger for "location", two fingers for "speed".
- Energy: warm, explaining something to a friend.

- On screen: `It keeps coming back.`
- Visual: To camera.

**0:15-0:25 · The setup**

> Here's what most people don't know. There are two completely different gut patterns that can cause this kind of all-day bloating, and they need completely different approaches.

- On screen: `Two different patterns.`
- Visual: Lean in slightly.

**0:25-0:37 · Reason one: location**

> The first is a location problem. Bacteria end up in a part of the gut where they don't belong. Every time you eat, they ferment the food right there, and the gas builds up through the day.

- On screen: `1. A location problem`
