I trained as a Functional Diagnostic Nutrition Practitioner because I was tired of watching women get handed something for a symptom nobody had bothered to explain.

Almost everyone I work with arrives having already been told their labs are normal. Several have been told it is stress, or hormones, or their age. A few have been told it is in their head.

Functional diagnostics looks at how your systems are performing rather than whether you have crossed a disease threshold. That is a different question, and it needs different testing to answer. It is also why I will tell you plainly when something belongs with your doctor rather than with me. I am not a physician and I do not diagnose.

What I do is find the pattern and build the work around it. This course is the first half of that: understanding the system well enough that the pattern, when you find it, actually means something to you.
