Spent years caring for kids, aging parents, or both. Her own health was always "later." Now bloating, fatigue, and brain fog are impossible to ignore. This may be the first time she is choosing herself. Highly motivated once trust is established.

## The Five Gut Archetypes

These are the segmentation categories from the ScoreApp quiz ("What's Your Gut Pattern?"). Every archetype has a locked analogy and a locked aha line. Use these consistently across all content, scripts, and copy.

### The Wired-and-Tired Gut
- **Pattern:** Nervous system / HPA axis dysregulation
- **Analogy:** Car with the engine timing off — every system is firing in the wrong sequence
- **Aha line:** "The food is fine. The system processing it isn't."

### The Reactive Gut
- **Pattern:** Inflammation / intestinal permeability
- **Analogy:** A damaged screen door — the filter is supposed to keep things out, but the holes are letting them through
- **Aha line:** "The foods aren't the problem. The barrier is."

### The Sugar-Driven Gut
- **Pattern:** Microbial overgrowth / candida
- **Analogy:** Squatters in the house — bacteria figured out how to manipulate the host into feeding them
- **Aha line:** "The cravings aren't yours. They're something else's, broadcasting through you."

### The Underactive Gut
- **Pattern:** Low stomach acid / hypochlorhydria
- **Analogy:** A kitchen with a stove that isn't hot enough — food sits there instead of cooking
- **Aha line:** "The problem isn't what you're eating. It's that your stomach isn't breaking it down."

### The Bloated and Backed-Up Gut
- **Pattern:** SIBO / bacterial relocation
- **Analogy:** Bacteria moved to the wrong neighborhood, plus a flooded basement with an unrepaired leak — the recurrence problem
- **Aha line:** "The bacteria aren't the problem. The location is."

**Clarified 2026-10-28: it is a COHORT, not an evergreen product.** A live

**Why a cohort cannot sit in an evergreen upsell slot.** The upsell fires
whenever someone buys, at any hour on any date. If the next cohort is six weeks
out, the buyer has been sold a wait; if a round has just finished, they have
been sold nothing for months. For an audience whose relationship with Alina is
built on not being misled, that is a bad first transaction, and it generates
refunds and support load.

**Where it belongs instead: the calendar, not the funnel.** Run it two or three
times a year as a campaign to the existing list. Its best job is
**reactivating $27 buyers who stalled**, which is a different group from the
buyers the ascension moves up. Best window is the first week of January rather
than before the holidays: that audience is most motivated and most receptive to
something short and finish-able right after the holidays, not during them.

**It is a lateral product, not a rung.** A narrow three-day protocol is not a
step up from the $27, and it is not a smaller Gut Reset Course either (the $147
is a broad system, this is one protocol). Treat the offer suite as a catalog
alongside the ladder: lateral products earn more from the same buyer without
requiring everyone to climb.

**The path to an evergreen version.** Run the live round, record it, and the
recording becomes a self-paced product with no dates attached, which *can* then
sit in the upsell slot. One production effort, two uses, and the live round
proves whether the content converts before the automated version gets built.

**Guthub carries a real marginal cost per user (recorded 2026-10-28).** It is an
AI platform, so every active user costs inference money. This makes it
structurally unlike every other rung: a course costs the same to deliver to one
buyer or a thousand, Guthub does not.

The consequence for funnel design, and the reason this is written down: **do not
propose giving Guthub away as a free bundle at the front of the funnel.** A
bundle pays inference for every buyer including the ones who never open it, which
puts maximum cost on the least committed users. A paid upsell or order bump
inverts that: the people generating cost are the ones who chose it and are
paying for it. This is a second, independent reason for the order-bump/upsell
placement Steve already favored.

The agent proposed bundling a free month of Guthub into the $147 on 2026-10-28

Gut Reset Course grants a **year** of access, so "bundle it for the included
period" would mean a year of inference per buyer. A Guthub window can be
decoupled from the course term (the $1,497 bundles three months), but the cost
objection stands regardless of the window.

## Compliance Guardrails

Alina is NOT a doctor and cannot diagnose conditions. All language uses pattern/archetype framing only.

**Never use:**
- "You have SIBO / Candida / Leaky Gut"
- Any direct medical diagnosis
- "Cure" or "treat"

**Always use:**
- "Your pattern suggests..."
- "This often shows up when..."
- "The [Archetype] pattern"
- "Address" or "work with" the pattern

**Clarified 2026-10-22 by Steve.** The ban is on *diagnosing the reader*, not on
the vocabulary. Naming a mechanism and explaining it is fine, and it is what
Alina does on camera.

- **Allowed:** "increased intestinal permeability, commonly called leaky gut",
  "the pattern suggests a barrier problem", explaining what the term means and
  where the evidence is thin.
- **Not allowed:** "you have leaky gut", "11 signs you have leaky gut", any claim
  to heal, seal, cure or treat it, and naming specific diseases as caused by it.

The agent had read this section as a blanket ban on the phrase and wrote around
it entirely, including cropping the words off a diagram. That put the written
course out of step with Alina's own recorded videos, which use the term
throughout. Explain the mechanism, never diagnose the reader.
