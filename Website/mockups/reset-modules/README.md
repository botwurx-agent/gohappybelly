## Text, the live risk on this set

Two of the nine were near-certain to break the no-text rule and both were
checked at native resolution rather than from thumbnails.

- **`mod-pattern-detail`.** Hand tools are normally stamped with maker's marks
  and sizes. Clean, and the folding rule's tick marks carry no legible numerals.
- **`mod-reference-shelf`.** Pantry jars attract labels and books attract
  spines. Jars are plain glass, spines are blank.

A fingerpost with blank arms (`mod-find-pattern`) is slightly unusual in life
but is the only way to have a signpost at all under the no-text rule, and it
reads correctly as a junction.

## Naming

`mod-` rather than `reset-module-`, deliberately, so nothing collides with the
cut-paper originals still in the library. Same reasoning as the `l03-` set.

`.png` is the 2752x1536 original, `.jpg` is 1600x893. `upload/` holds exactly
the nine files to upload and nothing else.
