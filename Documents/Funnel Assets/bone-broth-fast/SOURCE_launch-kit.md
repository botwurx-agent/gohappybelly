## 2. Program Outline

**Pre-Call Email** (sent immediately after purchase)
- Welcome + what to expect
- Grocery/broth-sourcing list (or your broth recipe)
- Reminder of kickoff call time + group link
- Reiterate the screening disclaimer

**Kickoff Zoom Call** (~30-40 min, day before or morning of Day 1)
1. Welcome + why a bone broth fast works (gut-lining rest, reduced inflammation, quick reset)
2. Exactly how to do it: how much broth/day, water, electrolytes, what you can/can't have
3. What's normal (hunger waves, mild headache day 1) vs. what's not (dizziness, fainting, stop and eat)
4. How to use the group for support
5. Live Q&A

**Day 1 Email/Voice Memo**
- Theme: "The hardest day is today, it gets easier."
- Reassurance + hydration reminder
- 1 quick tip (e.g., sip broth slowly, don't chug)
- Prompt: "Drop a comment in the group if you're in"

**Day 2 Email/Voice Memo**
- Theme: "This is where the magic happens."
- Normalize cravings dip / energy shift
- Encourage checking in with someone in the group
- Reminder of tomorrow's closing call time

**Day 3 Email/Voice Memo**
- Theme: "You made it, here's how to finish strong."
- Prep for breaking the fast (what to eat first, what to avoid)
- Reminder of closing call

**Closing Zoom Call** (~30 min, end of Day 3 or morning of Day 4)
1. Celebrate: quick round of "how do you feel" in chat/voice
2. How to break the fast over the next 24-48 hours
3. What to do with this momentum (soft pitch: 1:1 coaching / GI-MAP testing for anyone who wants to go deeper)
4. Open Q&A

## 3. Reels to Record (Launch Sequence)

Using existing formats (myth-bust, what-I-eat, symptom-cause-fix, personal story, Q&A/stitch, recipe):

1. **Personal story**: "Why I'm doing a 3-day bone broth fast (and why I'm not doing it alone this time)." Hook: "Last time I tried this, I quit by 2pm."
2. **Myth-bust**: "You don't need to starve to reset your gut." Bust the idea that fasting = suffering; explain what bone broth provides during a fast (minerals, collagen, gut-lining support).
3. **Symptom → cause → fix**: "Why you feel bloated even when you're 'eating healthy'" → ties into gut rest as the fix → soft mention of the reset.
4. **What I eat / recipe**: Show the actual broth (ingredients, simmer time). "This is what I'll be sipping for 3 days straight."
5. **Q&A / stitch**: Answer the top 3 questions people ask about fasting ("won't I lose muscle," "isn't this just detox nonsense," "what can I actually have"). Builds trust + handles objections before they buy.
6. **Behind-the-scenes / countdown**: "3 days until we start the reset together, here's exactly what's included." Direct CTA to link in bio.
7. **Day-of / results** (post-fast, once cohort finishes): Quick recap reel or duet of participant reactions (with permission) for social proof for the next round.

**Posting cadence:** Reels 1-3 about a week out, 4-5 a few days out, 6 the day before doors close, 7 after the cohort finishes (sets up the next round).
