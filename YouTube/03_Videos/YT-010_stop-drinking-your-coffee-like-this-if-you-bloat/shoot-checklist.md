# Shoot Day Checklist: YT-010 (STOP Drinking Your Coffee Like This If You Bloat)

Everything needed to film and upload this video. Print this or keep it open on set.

## 1. Before the shoot
- [ ] Alina reads the script out loud twice. Goal is familiarity, not memorization.
- [ ] Mark the 3 lines that must land word for word: the hook opener ("I am not going to tell you to quit coffee"), the aha line ("The food is fine. The system processing it isn't."), and the close ("Your coffee was never really the enemy. The timing was.").
- [ ] Wardrobe: solid, warm, on-brand tones (cream, sage, terracotta, navy). Avoid busy patterns and pure white or pure black. No large logos.
- [ ] Location: quiet room, soft daylight or a key light, a clean but warm background (not clinical). A coffee mug as a natural prop is fine here since it is on-topic; nothing medical.
- [ ] Water nearby, phone on silent, notifications off.
- [ ] Have an actual coffee mug and a small plate/bowl (for the Point 5 demo) on set and ready before rolling.

## 2. Gear and setup
- [ ] Camera at eye level, framed chest-up with a little headroom. Alina's eyes on the top third line.
- [ ] Lighting: main light slightly to one side and in front, softened (window with a sheer, or a softbox). Avoid overhead-only light.
- [ ] Audio is the priority. Use a lav mic or a shotgun mic close to her, not the camera's built-in mic. Record a 10-second silent room tone for the editor.
- [ ] Teleprompter: use a phone/tablet teleprompter app just below or over the lens so her eyeline stays on camera. Set scroll speed slow enough for natural pauses.
- [ ] Do a 20-second test recording. Check focus, exposure, and audio levels before the real take.

## 3. Delivery notes for Alina
- [ ] Talk to one person, not an audience. Imagine one specific woman who cannot function without her morning coffee and feels guilty every time she reads a "coffee is bad for you" headline.
- [ ] Say the "I am not telling you to quit coffee" line early with warmth and total conviction, not defensively. It needs to land as reassurance, not a disclaimer.
- [ ] Slow down on the hook and the aha line. Pauses read as confidence, not mistakes.
- [ ] Energy slightly higher than feels natural. Camera flattens energy by about 20 percent.
- [ ] It is fine to restart a sentence. Pause, clap once (helps the editor find the cut), and redo the line.
- [ ] Warm and direct, never clinical. This is a "here is what is actually happening" video, not a scare video.

## 4. Shot list (what to capture)
**A-roll (the main talking):** film each section as its own take: Hook, Credential, Problem, Point 1, Point 2, Point 3, Point 4, Point 5, Bridge/CTA, Outro.

**B-roll to grab (10 to 20 seconds each, silent):**
- [ ] Coffee pot pouring, a hand reaching for a mug first thing in the morning (hook)
- [ ] A rushed morning scene, an alarm going off, feet hitting the floor (problem block)
- [ ] A mug next to an empty plate, then a mug next to a small bowl of food (Point 2, also a clip)
- [ ] A tired woman scrolling her phone late at night, then reaching for coffee the next morning (Point 4)
- [ ] A small bite of food and a glass of water set next to the coffee mug (Point 5, also a clip)
- [ ] The engine-timing animation and the two-cortisol-lines graphic can be added in post, no need to film them.
