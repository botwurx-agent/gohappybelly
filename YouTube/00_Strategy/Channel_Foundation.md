### Recommendation

**Option 3: "Go Happy Belly with Alina Nazari"** (target handle @GoHappyBelly, with @GoHappyBellyFDNP or @AlinaNazariGutHealth as fallbacks if the primary is unavailable).

Reasoning:
- It inherits the existing brand equity: the Instagram account, the quiz, the offer names, and the email list already say "Go Happy Belly." A new viewer who has seen an IG Reel or the quiz results page recognizes the channel instantly.

The thumbnail face-plus-name combination (Section 2, thumbnail rules) does the rest of the personal-brand work on every single video regardless of what the channel name says.
- It avoids the two failure modes of the other options: a pure personal-brand name (option 1) orphans the existing IG/quiz brand recognition, and a pure business name (option 2) undersells the fact that Alina's face and credibility, not a logo, are what the playbook shows driving subscriptions in this niche.

## 2. Banner Concept

A concept brief for a designer, not final art. Uses the locked brand system only: Navy #1B2D4F, Sage Green #7BA987, Terracotta #C67B5C, Warm Cream #FAF6F0, White #FFFFFF, body text #374151, Georgia serif for all type.

**Canvas:** Standard YouTube banner safe-zone (desktop, tablet, mobile crop all considered; keep all text and the headshot inside the center safe area, 1235 x 338px).

**Layout, left to right:**
- **Left third:** Warm cream (#FAF6F0) background field. A close-crop, warm, direct-eye-contact photo of Alina (shoulders-up, natural light, not clinical), bleeding to the bottom edge of the banner so it reads as a personal, approachable presence rather than a stock photo.
- **Center:** Channel name set in Georgia serif, navy (#1B2D4F), large and bold: "Go Happy Belly" on one line, "with Alina Nazari, FDN-P" on a second, smaller line directly beneath in the same navy or in sage green (#7BA987) for a light hierarchy break.
- **Tagline, directly under the name block**, smaller Georgia serif, soft gray or navy: **"Root-cause answers for the gut symptoms nobody could explain."**
- **Right third:** A thin sage green (#7BA987) vertical divider line, then upload-cadence text in small caps or a simple line, navy on cream: "New videos every week." Optionally a small terracotta (#C67B5C) accent shape (a simple rounded rectangle or dot, not a busy graphic) near the divider to echo the terracotta CTA-button color used across email and funnel assets, so the banner feels visually related to the rest of the brand system without adding a call-to-action button (banners are not clickable).
- **Bottom strip (optional, thin):** A 4-6px sage green (#7BA987) rule across the full width, echoing the "sage accent line below logo" treatment already locked for the email template, so the YouTube banner visually rhymes with the email header.

**What NOT to include:** no clinical iconography (no diagnostic organ diagrams on the banner itself; that visual device is reserved for thumbnails per the playbook, Section 2, item 4, and even there stays non-diagnostic), no stock "healthy food flat-lay" imagery, no em dash anywhere in the tagline or cadence text, no diagnostic condition names.
