# Thumbnail Brief: What Happens If You Cut Sugar For 14 Days (YT-002)

- **Modeled on:** Ben Azadi thumbnail pattern (face plus strong expression, 2-4 word ALL-CAPS overlay, high contrast, one simple visual device), per Reference_Playbook Section 2. Rebuilt in the Go Happy Belly palette.
- **Big idea in one glance:** A no-sugar experiment with a payoff. The viewer should feel "wait, what does happen?" and want the answer.

## Text on Thumbnail
- **Primary text (4 words or fewer):** 14 DAYS, NO SUGAR
- **Secondary text (optional, 3 words or fewer):** WHAT HAPPENS?  (smaller, in terracotta)

## Visual Concept
- **Subject / expression:** Close-crop of Alina, shoulders up, an intrigued, slightly surprised "you are not going to believe this" expression. Direct eye contact with the lens.
- **Background / color:** Solid navy (#1B2D4F) ground so face and text pop. Primary text in warm cream/white with a subtle dark outline for grid legibility. Secondary text in terracotta (#C67B5C). A thin sage (#7BA987) accent used sparingly.
- **Visual device:** ONE simple prop or icon, not both. Option A (preferred): a small "no" ring over a sugar cube or a spoon of sugar, clean and bold. Option B: a simple 14-day calendar strip with a few days crossed off, low and small.

## Layout notes
- Face on the right ~45%, text stacked on the left third, device low and small.
- Test at phone-thumbnail size first (per playbook, mobile-first). If the words are not readable on a phone at a glance, cut a word.
- Keep the same face-framing, palette, and text treatment as YT-005 and future videos so the channel is recognizable in a grid.

## Compliance check
- [x] No diagnostic claims (no condition named, no "candida," no "cure/treat")
- [x] No banned words
- [x] No em dashes
- [x] Palette is on-brand (navy ground, cream/terracotta text, sage accent), not Ben's literal yellow/red
