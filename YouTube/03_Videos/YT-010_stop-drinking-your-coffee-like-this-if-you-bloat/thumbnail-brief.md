# Thumbnail Brief: STOP Drinking Your Coffee Like This If You Bloat (YT-010)

- **Modeled on:** Ben Azadi thumbnail pattern (face plus strong expression, 2-4 word ALL-CAPS overlay, high contrast, one simple visual device), per Reference_Playbook Section 2. Rebuilt in the Go Happy Belly palette.
- **Big idea in one glance:** Your coffee isn't the villain, how you're drinking it is. The viewer should feel "wait, I do that" and want the fix.

## Text on Thumbnail
- **Primary text (4 words or fewer):** IT'S NOT THE COFFEE
- **Secondary text (optional, 3 words or fewer):** FIX THE TIMING (smaller, in terracotta)

## Visual Concept
- **Subject / expression:** Close-crop of Alina, shoulders up, a knowing, slightly conspiratorial "let me tell you the real reason" expression, one eyebrow subtly raised. Direct eye contact with the lens.
- **Background / color:** Solid navy (#1B2D4F) ground so face and text pop. Primary text in warm cream/white with a subtle dark outline for grid legibility. Secondary text in terracotta (#C67B5C). A thin sage (#7BA987) accent used sparingly.
- **Visual device:** ONE simple prop or icon, not both. Option A (preferred): a coffee cup with a small clock/stopwatch face graphic overlaid or steaming where the steam forms a subtle clock hand, clean and bold, low and small. Option B: a simple coffee cup icon with a bold red "X" replaced by a small "timing" gear icon instead (avoid a plain "no coffee" ring, since the video is not anti-coffee and the thumbnail should not contradict that).

## Layout notes
- Face on the right ~45%, text stacked on the left third, coffee-cup device low and small.
- Test at phone-thumbnail size first (per playbook, mobile-first). If the words are not readable on a phone at a glance, cut a word.
- Keep the same face-framing, palette, and text treatment as YT-002 and YT-005 and future videos so the channel is recognizable in a grid.
- Do not use a "no" or crossed-out symbol directly over the coffee cup. The video's whole point is keep-the-coffee, fix-the-habit, and a crossed-out cup would send the opposite message before anyone clicks.

## Compliance check
- [x] No diagnostic claims (no condition named, no "adrenal fatigue," no "cure/treat")
- [x] No banned words
- [x] No em dashes
- [x] Palette is on-brand (navy ground, cream/terracotta text, sage accent), not Ben's literal yellow/red
