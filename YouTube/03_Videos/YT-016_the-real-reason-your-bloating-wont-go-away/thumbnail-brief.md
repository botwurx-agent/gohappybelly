# Thumbnail Brief: The Real Reason Your Bloating Won't Go Away (YT-016)

- **Modeled on:** Ben Azadi thumbnail pattern (face plus strong expression, 2-4 word ALL-CAPS overlay, high contrast, one simple visual device), per Reference_Playbook Section 2. Rebuilt in the Go Happy Belly palette.
- **Big idea in one glance:** This is not another "cut this food" video. Something else keeps bringing the bloating back, and this video finally names it. The viewer should feel "wait, that's why it keeps coming back" and click to find out what "it" is.

## Text on Thumbnail
- **Primary text (4 words or fewer):** IT'S NOT YOUR FOOD
- **Secondary text (optional, 3 words or fewer):** KEEPS COMING BACK (smaller, in terracotta)

## Visual Concept
- **Subject / expression:** Close-crop of Alina, shoulders up, direct eye contact, a knowing, steady, slightly conspiratorial expression, the look of someone about to tell you something nobody else has told you. Not shock, not alarm, resolve.
- **Background / color:** Solid navy (#1B2D4F) ground so face and text pop. Primary text in warm cream/white with a subtle dark outline for grid legibility. Secondary text in terracotta (#C67B5C). A thin sage (#7BA987) accent used sparingly.
- **Visual device:** ONE simple, non-clinical device. Preferred: a small circular "repeat" or cycle arrow icon (two curved arrows chasing each other), placed low and small, to visually cue "this keeps happening" without any organ or medical graphic. Alt option: a simple house-shaped outline with a small dot moving from one room to another, echoing the "wrong neighborhood" idea without reading as clinical.

## Layout notes
- Face on the right ~45%, text stacked on the left third, device low and small, beneath or beside the secondary text.
- Test at phone-thumbnail size first (per playbook, mobile-first). If the words are not readable on a phone at a glance, cut a word (drop to "NOT YOUR FOOD").
- Keep the same face-framing, palette, and text treatment as YT-002 and YT-005 so the channel is recognizable in a grid.

## Compliance check
- [x] No diagnostic claims (no condition named, no "SIBO," no "cure/treat")
- [x] No banned words
- [x] No em dashes
- [x] Palette is on-brand (navy ground, cream/terracotta text, sage accent), not Ben's literal yellow/red
