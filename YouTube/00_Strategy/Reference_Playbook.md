## 1. Title Formulas

Each formula below is proven on Ben Azadi's channel (real title, real view count, per `ben-azadi.md`) and translated into a non-diagnostic gut-health version in Alina's voice. Gut-health versions never name a condition as something the viewer "has." They use pattern language and, where relevant, one of the five locked archetypes.

### "What Happens When/If You [X]" (curiosity experiment)
- **Ben example:** "What Happens When You Stop Eating For 36 Hours" (2.6M views) / "What Happens If You ONLY Drink WATER For 72 Hours?" (2.3M views). *Per ben-azadi.md.*
- **Gut-health example:** "What Happens If You Stop Eating Sugar for 14 Days (Sugar-Driven Gut Pattern)"

### Numbered list ("[N] things to avoid / signs / rules")
- **Ben example:** "5 Butter Brands To AVOID At ALL Costs! (And 2 That WON'T Poison You)" (1.1M) / "7 Signs You're Losing Belly Fat (Even If the Scale Isn't Moving)" (263K). *Per ben-azadi.md.*
- **Gut-health example:** "7 Signs Your Bloating Keeps Coming Back for a Reason (Bloated-and-Backed-Up Gut Pattern)"

### "STOP [doing X]" imperative warning
- **Ben example:** "STOP Drinking These 5 Coffees, They're Quietly DESTROYING Your Gut & Hormones" (1.2M). *Per ben-azadi.md.*
- **Gut-health example:** "STOP Doing These 5 Things If Your Bloating Keeps Coming Back"

### Authority-hijack (credential stated before the claim)
- **Ben example:** "Harvard Doctor: Your Brain Is Starving for Creatine" (83K) / "This Heart Surgeon Has Performed Over 5,000 Heart Surgeries. THIS Is The ONLY Cause of Heart Disease" (74K). *Per ben-azadi.md.*
- **Gut-health example:** "FDN-P Practitioner: Why Your Labs Came Back Normal and You Still Feel Awful" (Alina's own credential stands in for the borrowed-authority slot; never invent a third party's diagnosis)

### Personal-experiment "I [did X] for [N] days"
- **Ben example:** "I Drank 30 TBSP of Olive Oil in 10 Days... Here's What Happened (Day 3 Wasn't Normal)" (1.4M) / "I Took 5g of Creatine for 30 Days... I Was Shocked by What Happened Next" (321K). *Per ben-azadi.md.*
- **Gut-health example:** "I Tried a 14-Day Digestion Support Protocol (Underactive Gut Pattern) - Here's What Happened"

### News-jacking (tying content to a live news event)
- **Ben example:** "36 Shocking Foods RFK Jr Just Banned" (4M) / "BREAKING: The FDA Just Recalled These Lettuce Products (Don't Eat Them!)" (574K). *Per ben-azadi.md.*
- **Gut-health example:** "The FDA Just Flagged These Additives, Here's What They Do to a Reactive Gut Pattern"

### Specific-percentage / specific-number claim
- **Ben example:** "You're Taking Creatine WRONG... This Shrinks Fat Cells by 30%" (1.8M) / "Blood Sugar Spikes Drop 52% If You Do THIS After Eating" (390K). *Per ben-azadi.md.*
- **Gut-health example:** "This One Change Cut My Client's Bloating in Half in 3 Weeks"

**Rule for all formulas:** the number, percentage, or day-count must be real and traceable to an actual client outcome, a cited study, or Alina's own trial. Never invent a statistic to fit a formula.

## 2. Thumbnail Rules

Adapted from the observed Ben Azadi thumbnail pattern (*per ben-azadi.md*, "Thumbnail Style" section) and the documented Carmichael thumbnail principles (*per jeremy-stickney-method.md*, items 7-8). Apply every rule below to Alina's thumbnails.

1. **Face plus a strong, legible expression, close up.** Ben's face appears in nearly every thumbnail with an animated expression. *Per ben-azadi.md.* Alina's thumbnails should use a close-crop shot of her face with a clear, readable emotion (concern, surprise, resolve) rather than a neutral headshot.
2. **2 to 4 word ALL-CAPS text overlay, large and bold.** Observed examples on Ben's channel: "THIS SH*T WORKS," "10 MIN LATER.," "FELT 30 AGAIN." *Per ben-azadi.md.* Alina's overlays should stay in the 2-4 word range and use pattern-safe language, for example "GUT ON FIRE" or "STOP THIS NOW," never a diagnostic phrase like "YOU HAVE SIBO."
3. **High-contrast palette: yellow, red, white text with a black outline for legibility at small sizes.** *Per ben-azadi.md.* Within Go Happy Belly's locked brand palette, use terracotta (#C67B5C) and warm cream/white as the high-contrast pairing, reserving a small red accent only for a "stop/warning" style thumbnail, so the system still reads as on-brand rather than copying Ben's exact yellow/red scheme.
4. **Recurring visual devices: organ graphics, before/after comparisons, prop shots.** Ben uses liver, pancreas, and fat-cell graphics plus before/after body photos and product props. *Per ben-azadi.md.* Alina's equivalent: simple, non-clinical gut/digestive-system line graphics (never a graphic implying a diagnosed disease state), or a before/after framed around symptom relief language ("bloated" versus "flat," not "diseased" versus "cured").
5. **Design for a small mobile thumbnail first, not a full-size image.** Bold, large, high-contrast text, avoid low-contrast or black-and-white images. *Per jeremy-stickney-method.md, item 8.* Test every thumbnail at the size it will actually render in a suggested-videos grid before finalizing.
6. **Keep a consistent visual identity across every thumbnail** (same color family, same face-framing style, same text treatment) so the channel is recognizable in a grid without reading the title. *Per jeremy-stickney-method.md, item 7.*

## 3. Cadence & Format (locked)

**This is a locked decision, confirmed by Steve.** Alina goes solo-first. Long guest interviews are explicitly not the format for this channel.

- **Target: 2 long-form uploads per week**, achieved through batch-filming multiple videos in a single sitting rather than filming one video per week in isolation. This mirrors the documented Carmichael batch-recording principle (*per jeremy-stickney-method.md, item 15*) and sits within the "1-3 long-form uploads per week when daily isn't realistic" floor Carmichael describes (*per jeremy-stickney-method.md, item 4*).
- **Format: solo, punchy, single-topic videos, 16 to 27 minutes.** This range and this format are the proven high performers on the anchor channel: Ben's solo educational videos in this length range make up the majority of his uploads and are consistently his top performers. *Per ben-azadi.md, "Cadence & Format" section.*
- **Do NOT do long guest interviews.** On the anchor channel, guest interviews (50-63 minutes, named experts) consistently underperform, landing at 6.9K-13K views at 2-4 weeks old versus 50K-500K+ for solo uploads in the same window, roughly a 5-10x gap in the same time window. *Per ben-azadi.md, "Cadence & Format" section.* Alina's channel should not invest production time in this format while it is building watch history and channel authority.
- **One idea per video.** Every long-form video should fully answer the question in its title, mapped to a single archetype or avatar, before any mention of a next step. This is consistent with the "education before the pitch" principle (*per jeremy-stickney-method.md, item 13*) and with the funnel architecture already in place (content leads to quiz leads to call).
- **Batching is the mechanism, not the exception.** Because Alina is not personally running the channel day to day, hitting 2/week reliably requires filming several scripts back to back in one sitting, consistent with the "batch-record multiple videos in a single sitting" principle. *Per jeremy-stickney-method.md, item 15.*

## 4. Hook Patterns (hypotheses to test)

**Important caveat, stated plainly:** no video transcripts were captured for the Ben Azadi teardown, only titles, view counts, and thumbnail observations. The patterns below are informed inference from title structure, explicitly flagged as a starting hypothesis to test, not a confirmed observation. *Per ben-azadi.md, "Hook Patterns" section.*

1. **Restate the title's question on camera before any explanation.** A curiosity-experiment title like "What Happens If You Stop Eating Sugar for 14 Days" should open with Alina asking that exact question to camera before unpacking it. *Hypothesis, per ben-azadi.md inference.*
2. **Front-load the anomaly or twist, not the topic.** Personal-experiment videos should open with the unexpected result ("by day 3, something happened I didn't expect") rather than explaining the premise first. *Hypothesis, per ben-azadi.md inference.*
3. **Front-load the credential before the claim.** Authority-hijack style hooks should state Alina's FDN-P credential (or a cited practitioner's, always attributed and never fabricated) before the claim that follows. *Hypothesis, per ben-azadi.md inference.*
4. **Name the news event directly.** News-jacking videos should open by naming the specific story (an FDA action, a food-policy headline) before pivoting to the gut-health lesson. *Hypothesis, per ben-azadi.md inference.*
5. **Open with the avatar's exact frustration, stated in her words.** This one is documented rather than inferred: Carmichael's "hook as an advertisement for the rest of the video" principle, applied here as opening with the target avatar's specific symptom or frustration (for example, Avatar 1, The Dismissed Patient: "your labs came back normal, and you still feel awful") before any explanation. *Per jeremy-stickney-method.md, item 5, and its Go Happy Belly application section.*

All five patterns should be treated as testable hypotheses. Track retention on the first 60 seconds of each video and revise this section once real data exists.

## 5. Overperforming Topic Categories

Ben Azadi's proven-demand categories (*per ben-azadi.md, "Topic Categories That Overperform"*), mapped to gut-health where a credible, non-diagnostic bridge exists.

| Ben's category (proven) | Gut-health mapping | Archetype fit |
|---|---|---|
| Fasting / water fasting | Digestion resets, structured eating-window trials | Underactive Gut, Sugar-Driven Gut |
| Visceral and belly fat | Bloating, "belly" content (distinct from fat loss framing, kept in digestive-symptom language) | Bloated-and-Backed-Up Gut |
| Insulin and blood sugar | Blood sugar and cravings content | Sugar-Driven Gut |
| Cortisol | Cortisol, sleep, and stress-and-digestion content | Wired-and-Tired Gut |
| "Brands to avoid" consumer-safety format | Gut-irritating additive categories, supplement quality red flags | Reactive Gut |
| News-jacking food policy (RFK Jr, FDA recalls) | Food-policy or food-safety news relevant to gut health | Cross-archetype, use carefully (see below) |
| Creatine / supplement deep-dives | Gut-relevant supplement content (probiotics, digestive enzymes, HCl support) | Underactive Gut, Reactive Gut |
| Anti-aging and skin | Skin flare-ups as a downstream gut signal (ties to Avatar 2, Burned-Out Professional) | Reactive Gut |

**News-jacking caution:** Ben's RFK Jr and FDA-recall videos are among his single biggest hits, but news-jacking content reaches cold, skeptical audiences who scrutinize medical claims closely. Every pivot back to Alina's content must stay in "this pattern often shows up when" language and avoid "cure" or "treat." *Per ben-azadi.md, "Gut-Health Adaptation Notes."*

## 6. Method Principles (from Jeremy Stickney / Evan Carmichael)

Sourced from `jeremy-stickney-method.md`. Attribution note up front: everything below is documented as part of the Carmichael system specifically (public interviews, podcast recaps, Carmichael's own sales materials). The claim that Jeremy Stickney's coaching of Ben Azadi follows this system is inference, supported by the fact that Ben's actual channel behavior matches these principles closely, but not a confirmed transcript of Jeremy coaching Ben. Each item below is marked Documented (the Carmichael-system principle itself is documented) or Inference (the link to Ben/Jeregy specifically is inferred).

1. **Model proven outliers, don't guess at topics.** Find videos overperforming their channel's normal subscriber-to-view ratio, then build an Alina-original video on the same underlying question with her own angle. "Success leaves clues." *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 1.*
2. **Write titles by studying what already works, not freehand.** Reverse-engineer proven title structures (emotional trigger, specific-number claim, curiosity gap) rather than inventing titles from scratch. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 2.*
3. **Recommendation engine is the primary growth lever, search is secondary but not ignored.** Package videos to sit naturally next to existing high-performing gut-health content, while still including plain-language search terms a symptomatic viewer would type ("why am I bloated after eating," "always tired gut"). Note: sources show real tension on this point, one says recommendation dominates, another frames YouTube as a search engine for evergreen content; both are worth designing for. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 3.*
4. **Consistency beats polish, up to a point.** A repeatable weekly production system that reliably outputs 2 long-form videos a week beats waiting for a "perfect" video. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 4; applied to Go Happy Belly's cadence in Section 3 above.*
5. **Hook in the first seconds like it's an ad for the rest of the video.** See Section 4, pattern 5. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 5.*
6. **Favor long-form (10-20+ minutes) over short, thin videos.** Matches the 16-27 minute range already proven on Ben's channel. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 6; corroborated by observed data in ben-azadi.md.*
7. **Build a consistent visual identity so the channel is recognizable in a suggested-videos grid.** See Thumbnail Rules, item 6. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 7.*
8. **Design thumbnails for a small mobile screen first.** See Thumbnail Rules, item 5. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 8.*
9. **Track retention, treat the curve as directional data.** Documented target: roughly 70% retention at the one-minute mark. Spikes mark the strongest moments, the ones to clip for repurposing. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 9.*
10. **Cut every long-form video into multiple shorter pieces for other platforms**, prioritizing the highest-retention segments, ideally the moment an archetype's locked aha line lands (for example, "The foods aren't the problem. The barrier is."). *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 10.*
11. **Direct viewers to one specific next video at the end of every video**, via end screens and pinned comments, so a session chains into multiple views. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 11.*
12. **Reply to comments.** Viewers who get an on-camera-creator reply are roughly seven times more likely to subscribe, per Carmichael-linked sources. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 12.*
13. **Lead every video with education, not a pitch.** Fully answer the title's question before any mention of the Gut Breakthrough Call, the quiz, or Guthub. "You have to earn the right to sell something to somebody." *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 13; matches Go Happy Belly's existing funnel architecture (organic content leads to quiz leads to call).*
14. **Play to personal strengths, cut what isn't a strength.** Structure the format around whatever plays to Alina's actual on-camera strengths rather than forcing a mismatched format. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 14.*
15. **Batch-record multiple videos in one sitting** to sustain cadence without a new setup or mindset every session. This is the mechanism behind the 2/week target in Section 3. *Documented, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 15.*
16. **Client-derived content requires explicit consent and full de-identification.** The Carmichael-documented tactic of repurposing 1:1 coaching sessions into content does not transfer directly to a health practice; any use of real client material requires explicit consent and de-identification as a hard requirement, not an optional nicety. *Documented tactic, flagged adaptation, per jeremy-stickney-method.md item 16.*
17. **The Jeremy-to-Ben lineage itself.** The working assumption that Jeremy's coaching of Ben Azadi is a refinement of the Carmichael system above is inference, not a confirmed transcript. It is supported by the pattern-match between Ben's documented channel behavior (near-daily uploads, solo videos outperforming guest interviews, heavy thumbnail/title consistency) and the Carmichael principles. *Inference, per jeremy-stickney-method.md, "Why his method matters for this project."*

## 7. The Packaging Checklist

Run this checklist on every new video idea before it gets scripted or filmed.

- [ ] **Title uses a proven formula** (curiosity-experiment, numbered list, STOP, authority-hijack, personal-experiment, news-jacking, or specific-percentage), per Section 1.
- [ ] **Any number, percentage, or day-count in the title is real** and traceable to a client outcome, a cited study, or an actual Alina trial, not invented to fit the formula.
- [ ] **Thumbnail plan includes a face with a clear expression, a 2-4 word ALL-CAPS overlay, and the locked brand palette** (terracotta/cream/navy, per Section 2), reviewed at mobile-thumbnail size before finalizing.
- [ ] **One clear idea.** The video fully answers one question, mapped to one archetype or avatar, no scope creep into a second topic.
- [ ] **Maps to an archetype and/or avatar.** Named explicitly in the planning notes (for example, "Bloated-and-Backed-Up Gut, Avatar 4 The Frustrated Dieter") so the locked analogy and aha line can be used consistently.
- [ ] **Topic sits in a proven-demand category** from Section 5, or is a deliberate, clearly-labeled test outside those categories.
- [ ] **Format is solo, 16-27 minutes.** No guest-interview format, per the locked cadence decision in Section 3.
- [ ] **Non-diagnostic language check.** No "you have [condition]," no "cure," no "treat." Only "your pattern suggests," "this often shows up when," "the [Archetype] pattern," "address" or "work with" the pattern.
- [ ] **No em dashes, no banned words** (transform, journey, empower, authentic, passionate, innovative, creative in the marketing sense, unique, elevate, synergy).
- [ ] **Education comes before any pitch.** The Gut Breakthrough Call, the quiz, or Guthub is mentioned only after the title's question is fully answered.
- [ ] **A next-video and a clipping plan exist.** One specific end-screen link identified, and the segment likely to carry the highest retention (ideally where the archetype's aha line lands) flagged for Shorts/Reels/TikTok repurposing.
