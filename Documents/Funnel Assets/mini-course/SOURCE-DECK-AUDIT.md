## Two factual problems

1. **The immune figure contradicts itself.** Slide 13: "Over 70% of your immune
   system lives in your gut." Slide 22: "70-80% of immune cells reside in the
   gut." Same claim, two numbers, same deck.
2. **The serotonin claim appears twice and is shaky.** Slides 21 and 61 both say
   "90% of serotonin is made in the gut," both as the reason the gut affects
   mood. Gut serotonin is overwhelmingly peripheral and does not cross the blood
   brain barrier, so the figure does not support the conclusion drawn from it.
   Duplicate content and a weak claim in one.

## What is genuinely good about it

**It was built to hand off to a practitioner.** The final slide is "Next Steps
with Your Practitioner: identify remaining imbalances, explore functional
testing if needed, personalize your long-term plan," followed by a Call to
Action placeholder. The ascension step is designed in.

**Functional testing is named three times** as the next step, on slides 64, 76
and 84. Three built-in bridges to the GI-MAP and the $1,497, already in the
source, needing no insertion.

**It does not compete with the $147 course.** This deck is general gut
education. The Gut Reset Course is pattern-specific: five archetypes, four
weeks, personalized. "Understand how your gut works" ascending to "find and work
your specific pattern" is a clean ladder, and the upsell line writes itself.

## The real question: is this worth $47 as it stands

No. 2,229 words of bullets, no video, no workbook, no personalization, against a
$147 course with four weeks of structure, a quiz, a tracker and five pattern
paths. A buyer who sees both will feel it.

What is missing is Alina. The content is a serviceable spine; the value would
come from her voice over it. Recorded audio or video per module, her analogies,
her corrections where the deck is weak, turns a $47 price into a fair one.

## Blocked on

**FDN's license, specifically whether modification is permitted.** The
compliance pass is not a light edit. It renames two modules, rewrites four
slides built on "leaky gut," and removes a claim on slide 34. If the material
must ship unmodified, it cannot ship at all under CLAUDE.md, and the plan needs
to change rather than the deck.
