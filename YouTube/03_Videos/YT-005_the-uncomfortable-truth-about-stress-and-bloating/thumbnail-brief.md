# Thumbnail Brief: The Uncomfortable Truth About Stress and Bloating Nobody Tells You (YT-005)

- **Modeled on:** Ben Azadi thumbnail pattern (face plus strong expression, 2-4 word ALL-CAPS overlay, high-contrast, a simple visual device), per Reference_Playbook Section 2. Adapted to Alina's locked brand palette.
- **Big idea in one glance:** It is not the food, it is the stress. The viewer should feel "wait, my bloating is from stress?" in under a second.

## Text on Thumbnail
- **Primary text (4 words or fewer):** NOT YOUR FOOD
- **Secondary text (optional, 3 words or fewer):** IT'S STRESS  (smaller, beneath or beside the primary line, in terracotta)

## Visual Concept
- **Subject / expression:** Close-crop of Alina, shoulders up, a knowing, slightly concerned "let me tell you the truth" expression (not a smile, not fear). One hand can rest on her stomach to signal the topic without being clinical. Direct eye contact with the lens.
- **Background / color:** Solid or softly textured navy (#1B2D4F) ground so the face and text pop. Primary text in warm cream/white with a subtle dark outline for grid legibility. Secondary text in terracotta (#C67B5C). A thin sage (#7BA987) accent line or shape can echo the brand, used sparingly.
- **Visual device:** A simple, non-clinical cue that ties stress to the gut. Option A (preferred): a small, clean icon of a tangled or "revved" line settling into a calm wave, placed low, suggesting the engine-timing idea without a medical organ diagram. Option B: a subtle contrast of a plate of food with a faint red "no" slash, paired with a small stress squiggle near her head. Keep it to ONE device, not both.

## Layout notes
- Face occupies roughly the right 45 percent, text stacked on the left third, device low and small. Test at phone-thumbnail size first (per playbook, mobile-first). If the words are not readable on a phone at a glance, cut a word.
- Keep the same face-framing, palette, and text treatment as future thumbnails so the channel is recognizable in a grid.
