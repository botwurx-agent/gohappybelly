# Thumbnail Brief: The Easiest Way to Reduce Bloating Without a Restrictive Diet (YT-012)

- **Modeled on:** Ben Azadi thumbnail pattern (face plus strong expression, 2-4 word ALL-CAPS overlay, high contrast, one simple visual device), per Reference_Playbook Section 2. Rebuilt in the Go Happy Belly palette.
- **Big idea in one glance:** Bloating relief without another restrictive diet. The viewer should feel relief, "wait, I don't have to cut anything else out?" and click to find out what the one habit is.

## Text on Thumbnail
- **Primary text (4 words or fewer):** BLOAT-FREE, NO DIET
- **Secondary text (optional, 3 words or fewer):** ONE SIMPLE HABIT (smaller, in terracotta)

## Visual Concept
- **Subject / expression:** Close-crop of Alina, shoulders up, warm and relieved, an "I've got good news" half-smile, direct eye contact with the lens. Lighter and more upbeat than a "shocking discovery" expression, this thumbnail should read as relief, not alarm.
- **Background / color:** Solid navy (#1B2D4F) ground so face and text pop. Primary text in warm cream/white with a subtle dark outline for grid legibility. Secondary text in terracotta (#C67B5C). A thin sage (#7BA987) accent used sparingly, for example a small checkmark or underline beneath the primary text.
- **Visual device:** ONE simple, non-clinical device. Option A (preferred): a plate icon with a small clock or timer beside it, simple line art, communicating "when you eat," not "what you eat." Option B: a crossed-out food restriction list icon (a small clipboard with an X through it) to visually reinforce "no restrictive diet."

## Layout notes
- Face on the right ~45%, text stacked on the left third, device low and small.
- Test at phone-thumbnail size first (per playbook, mobile-first). If the words are not readable on a phone at a glance, cut a word.
- Keep the same face-framing, palette, and text treatment as YT-002 and YT-005 so the channel is recognizable in a grid.
- Do not use a "before/after body" style graphic here, that reads as a weight-loss thumbnail, not a bloating-relief one. Keep the device tied to eating timing or "no restriction," not body transformation.

## Compliance check
- [x] No diagnostic claims (no condition named, no "SIBO," no "cure/treat")
- [x] No banned words
- [x] No em dashes
- [x] Palette is on-brand (navy ground, cream/terracotta text, sage accent), not Ben's literal yellow/red
