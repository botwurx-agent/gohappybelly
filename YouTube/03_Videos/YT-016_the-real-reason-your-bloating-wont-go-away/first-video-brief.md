### Why YT-016 wins
1. **It is the channel's thesis.** A stranger's first video should tell them exactly who this channel is for. "Normal labs, still bloated, you did not fail the diet" is Alina's whole practice in one sentence, and it is the same promise as the banner and About copy in `Channel_Foundation.md`.
2. **It targets the avatar who matters most.** Avatar 1 has the highest urgency and is the main buyer of the $1,497 3-Month Gut Reset. Phase 1 is grow-first (no pitch), but the first viewers we attract should be the right ones.
3. **Best title of the four.** 43 characters, hook front-loaded, survives mobile truncation. YT-005's title breaks our own 60-character rule.
4. **It builds a working two-video chain on day one.** YT-016's outro hands off to YT-005, and both get filmed on the same batch day. YT-005 first would point at a video that does not exist yet.
5. **Bloating is the biggest door.** It's the most searched symptom in our keyword list, and three of our four ready videos are about it, so a viewer who likes video #1 has a library to binge.

### What changed in the script (v2)
- **Runtime fix (the big one, and it affects the whole library).** The v1 script was labeled 17 to 19 minutes but had about 1,740 spoken words, which is roughly **12 minutes** on camera. The playbook locks 16 to 27 minutes. v2 is about 2,220 spoken words, about **16 to 17 minutes** finished. New material: a "what a reset day looks like" walkthrough, a new Point 6 (3 mistakes that keep the cycle going, with a new retention loop), and a recap.
- **Hook cut from about 60 seconds to about 35.** The playbook target is 70% of viewers still watching at the one-minute mark. A long hook burns that.
- **Compliance tightened.** "Your bacteria have relocated" became "one pattern I see over and over." A red-flag safety line was added ("blood in your stool, weight loss you cannot explain, a fever, or severe pain, see your doctor first"). The description now carries a "not medical advice, not a doctor" line.
- **No unconfirmed claims.** Four statements about Alina's own clients are marked **[ALINA CONFIRM]**. The playbook rule is that any client outcome must be real. Alina confirms or we cut.
