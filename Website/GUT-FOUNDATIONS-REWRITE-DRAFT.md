**AFTER**

> GUT FOUNDATIONS · $27
> # Why the same gut advice works for her but not for you.
> Kombucha sorted your friend out. It bloats you for two days. Fiber helps one person and backs another one up. The advice is not wrong. It is just not written for the gut you have.
> There are five common patterns, and they want opposite things. That is why everything you read contradicts everything else you read. Eleven short lessons walk you through all five, so you can tell which one your week actually looks like.

**Why.** The headline stays; it is the strongest line on the page. The second

which is the reason the advice conflicts. It was sitting further down the page
doing nothing. A guarantee belongs above the fold, as a reason to keep reading,
which is what the competitor does.

## 2. The drawer

**BEFORE**

> ## You have a drawer of half-finished bottles
> The probiotic with the good reviews. The digestive enzymes. The magnesium someone in a Facebook group swore by. Most of them are two-thirds full.
> *(...)*
> None of that was a discipline problem. You were running experiments without knowing what you were testing.

**AFTER**

> ## It was never a discipline problem
> You have a drawer of half-finished bottles. The probiotic with the good reviews. The digestive enzymes. The magnesium someone in a Facebook group swore by. Most of them are two-thirds full.
> You have taken out dairy, then gluten, then nightshades. The safe list keeps getting shorter and the bloating finds you anyway. You know which jeans not to wear after lunch.
> And because you changed three things in the same week, you still cannot say which one did anything.
> You were running experiments without knowing what you were testing. That is a method problem, and method problems are fixable.

**Why.** The argument was buried in the last line. Promote it to the heading so
the section makes a claim, and let the drawer open the body instead. The closing
line now points forward rather than just landing.

**Why this is the biggest missing piece.** Nothing on any of our three pages
says what it costs to do nothing. The competitor devotes two sections to it.

**And why this version is compliant.** The stakes are about her *method*, not
her body. It does not say her symptoms will worsen, name a disease, or predict
anything physiological. It says that guessing stays unreadable, which is a
statement about process and is simply true.

## 4. What changes  (prose becomes cards)

**BEFORE.** Three paragraphs plus a four-item list. The content is right and
specific. It is buried in prose at roughly 10% of the page.

**AFTER.** Keep the opening paragraph, then six cards:

> AFTER THE ELEVEN LESSONS
> ## What changes is that you stop guessing
> You will not finish this with a protocol. You will finish it able to look at a bad afternoon and say what probably caused it, which is the thing you have not been able to do yet.
>
> **01 Name your pattern** · Say which of the five your week actually looks like, and why that one.
> **02 Read a bad day** · Look back at an afternoon that went wrong and say what probably set it off.
> **03 Spot advice that is not for you** · Read a gut health post and tell whether it was written for your pattern or somebody else's.
> **04 Run one test at a time** · Change one thing and actually learn from it, rather than three at once.
> **05 Stop the drawer filling** · Buy on reasoning instead of on a recommendation written for a different pattern.
> **06 Ask a sharper question** · Walk into your next appointment knowing what to ask about.
>
> *You cannot work with a pattern you have not named.*
> **[ Get Gut Foundations, $27 ]**

**Why.** Same substance, absorbed in two seconds instead of ninety. Every card
is something **she** will be able to do, not something her body will do, so
there is no outcome claim anywhere in it.

## Compliance check

No em dashes. None of the banned words. No diagnosis of the reader, no claim
to heal, seal, cure or treat, no disease named, no prediction about anybody's
body. Every outcome is phrased as something the reader will be able to do.
Pattern language throughout.
