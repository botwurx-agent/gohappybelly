I want to be straightforward with you about this up front, because it matters.

I am a Functional Diagnostic Nutrition Practitioner. I am not a medical doctor. I do not diagnose conditions and I do not prescribe. What I do is look at patterns, the way symptoms cluster and what tends to sit underneath them, and help people work with the pattern they are in.

So you will notice I never say "you have" anything in this course. I say "this pattern often shows up when." That is not hedging. It is the honest description of what can be known from the outside.

Taking this course does not make you my client, and nothing here replaces individual medical care. If you take a medication, decisions about it belong with the person who prescribed it. If something in here raises a question about your own health, that is a good thing, and the right next step is a conversation, not a guess.
