## Why this wording

**"Start with the pattern you just got"** connects the purchase to the thing the
reader is holding right now. The quiz gave them a label; the natural next
question is what it means. That is exactly what the $27 answers, so the offer
reads as the continuation rather than as an upsell.

**"what all five patterns look like"** is doing quiet work. It tells a reader
who half suspects the quiz guessed wrong that she will see the other four and
can place herself. That removes the objection without arguing with the result.

**No urgency, no scarcity, no countdown.** Nothing on this page should imply
pressure. The reader has just been told something about her body by someone she
met two minutes ago. Pressure here reads as a pitch and costs the trust the
quiz just earned.

**The call keeps a real heading, not a demoted one.** "Would you rather talk it
through?" treats it as a genuine alternative for someone who wants it. A reader
who was always going to book will still book.

Compliance: pattern language throughout, nothing diagnostic, no claim to heal,
seal, cure or treat, no em dashes, none of the banned words.

## The risk Steve accepted, recorded

The Gut Breakthrough Call is currently the only mechanism selling the $1,497
3-Month Gut Reset, because that sales page does not exist yet. Demoting the call
on the results pages could cost call bookings, and one $1,497 sale is worth 55
sales of the $27.

Steve's position, 2026-10-28: the $27 is the low-ticket front door and the offer
to sell at scale, with everything else up the ladder from it. Recorded here so
the tradeoff is visible if call volume drops rather than being rediscovered.

**The piece that makes this safe is the ascension, and it does not exist yet.**
A Gut Foundations buyer currently reaches a thank-you page and is sold nothing
further. Until a $27 buyer is moved toward the $147 and the $1,497, the front
door collects buyers it does not pass on. That is the next thing to build, and
it matters more than any further CTA placement.
